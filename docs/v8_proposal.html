V8 Proposal — Two-Pass Causal Token-Conditioned Flow

Every measured result in this project points to one mechanism: draft positions accept when they are conditioned on the actual tokens before them. V3 tried this bidirectionally and failed; EAGLE does it fully-causally and wins but abandons flow and pays K sequential passes; V6 does it cheaply at the logit level and crosses 1× but plateaus — because the underlying hiddens are still predicted blind (base CE ≈ 1.1 vs corrected ≈ 0.22). V8 repairs the hidden itself: a second, short, causally token-conditioned velocity pass initialized at pass-1's output.

V3 — bidirectional refiner (tested: lost)

Noise-init block refined N steps; each step re-decodes and re-conditions bidirectionally — position i sees estimates of its own and future positions. Ill-posed + leaky teacher-forcing, long noisy transport. Lost to EAGLE on every domain and was slower.

EAGLE — full causal chain (tested: best overall)

Not a flow. One net applied K times sequentially; each step consumes the previous predicted hidden and its decoded token's embedding. Exact prefix conditioning at every depth — but K sequential drafter passes, and it abandons the flow framework.
greedy 3.21 · acc@4 0.57 · live 1.02×

V6 — Markov flow (tested: best flow, plateaued)

One parallel flow pass; a rank-256 bigram head adds a token→logit bias left-to-right at decode. First flow >1× live — but it corrects logits only: the deep-position hiddens are still predicted blind (base CE ≈ 1.1 vs corrected ≈ 0.22), and V7's order-2/gating/epochs all confirmed the plateau.
greedy 2.93 · live 1.003×acc@4 0.47 · plateaued (V7 ≈ V6)

V8 — two-pass causal flow (proposed)

Pass 1 = V6's parallel flow (noised-delta init). Decode provisional tokens t¹. Pass 2 = one short velocity pass, initialized at pass-1's hiddens, causally conditioned on emb(t¹<i) — repairs the hidden itself with the actual draft prefix. Optional: batch N pass-1 samples, keep the best (flow-native "more predictions").
projected: greedy ~3.1–3.3 · live ~1.05×+ best-of-N on same checkpoint

Why V8 should work, from our own measurements: (1) token feedback is the proven lever (EAGLE +0.53 greedy; Markov +0.25) and V8 applies it at the hidden level, where V6's residual deficit lives (base-flow CE ≈ 1.1 vs bias-corrected ≈ 0.22 — the head compensates for hiddens that are wrong). (2) The V3 failure modes are removed by construction: strictly causal conditioning (position i sees only t¹<i — teacher-forceable, leak-impossible) and a short residual transport from pass-1's already-good hiddens instead of a long trajectory from noise. (3) On-policy training is free because pass 1 is parallel: during training, pass 2 conditions on pass 1's own argmax tokens (no-grad) — the model learns to correct its actual mistakes, at zero sequential cost (EAGLE cannot do this cheaply). (4) Mechanically: pass-2's position 4 has EAGLE-equivalent information whenever the pass-1 prefix is correct (~66% at position 3), lower-bounding the expected gain. Cost: one extra integration + decode ≈ +4 ms on a ~35 ms step. The noised-delta init additionally turns the drafter into a sampler, enabling batched best-of-N draft selection — "more predictions" — on the same trained checkpoint.
